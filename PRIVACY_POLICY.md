# Doc-Inspector-HP — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 5, 2026

Doc-Inspector-HP is a browser extension intended to help reservation support agents improve the spelling, punctuation, and readability of notes they enter on HotelPlanner pages.

## Data processed

When enabled on a supported page, the extension reads text from the agent's selected notes field to display a live preview and suggest optional corrections. Those notes may include customer, booking, or contact information entered by the agent. The extension processes that text locally in the browser using bundled rules and grammar components.

## Use and control

The extension uses note text only to generate writing suggestions. Agents can review, accept, or reject proposed edits. It does not submit notes, make booking changes, initiate refunds, or perform cancellations. Agents can stop, minimize, or hide the writing assistant.

## Storage and sharing

The extension does not intentionally upload, transmit, or store note text on its own servers, third-party servers, or analytics services. It does not sell note content or use it for advertising. Its user-configurable preferences, such as approved abbreviation additions and writing settings, are saved using Chrome extension storage (including Chrome Sync when enabled). Chrome may synchronize those preferences under the user's browser account. Note content is not saved in those preferences.

## Permissions and site access

On HotelPlanner pages, the extension displays an in-page writing assistant. The extension also supports user-initiated activation on a selected website tab. The permissions `activeTab`, `scripting`, and `storage` support those functions. No external website or cloud AI service is required to process the text.

## Security and limitations

The extension's offline proofreading tools may occasionally produce incorrect suggestions. Agents are responsible for reviewing changes, especially guest names, booking numbers, refund or cancellation statuses, and other sensitive booking information, before accepting. Avoid placing full payment-card details or passwords in reservation notes.

## Policy changes

If the extension's handling of note text or other data materially changes, this policy and the extension's disclosures should be updated before that change is released.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the support contact shown on the extension's Chrome Web Store listing.
